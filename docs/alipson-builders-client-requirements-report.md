## A9. File delivery and approval

All files should come through one shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder, organised as in section E. Please do not send website photos over WhatsApp.

1. **One shared folder.** Use Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, with edit access for the web team. Use the folder layout in section E.
2. **Originals only.** Send the original camera or phone file, not screenshots or WhatsApp-forwarded copies. WhatsApp compresses images to about 1600 px and removes detail.
3. **Minimum quality.** Photos at least 2000 px on the long side, in JPG, HEIC or RAW. Videos 1080p or higher, in MP4 or MOV, or a YouTube or Vimeo link.
4. **File names.** Name files by project and number, e.g. `grandeur-ernakulam-01.jpg`.
5. **Text in one document.** Put all written answers in one Google Doc or Word file (`00_Answers`), numbered to match the questions in section D.
6. **Permissions.** Put signed customer and property-owner consents in `09_Permissions`.
7. **Final sign-off.** After the site is updated, the client reviews it on a preview link and confirms by email before launch.

Suggested turnaround: High-priority items within 7 days. Everything else within 14 days.
