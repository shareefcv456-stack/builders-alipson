Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

This policy explains what Alipson Builders Private Limited (“Alipson Builders”, “we”) does with the information you give us through this website. It covers this site only.

1. What we collect

We collect only what you type into the enquiry form:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your phone number;
  • the description of your project that you write in the message field.

We do not ask for identity documents, payment details or any special category of personal data through this website, and you should not send them through the enquiry form.

2. Why we use it

To answer your enquiry: to call or email you back, to discuss scope and budget, and to prepare an estimate if you ask for one. We do not sell your details, and we do not share them with anyone outside Alipson Builders except where a consultant or contractor working on your own project needs them to do that work.

3. Marketing

We contact you about the enquiry you sent. If we ever want to send you anything else, we will ask you first, and you can tell us to stop at any time by replying to any message or writing to the address below.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are kept while the conversation is live and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back to us. Records connected to a project we deliver are kept for as long as the law and our contractual obligations require.

5. Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies and does not track you across other websites. Some pages embed a Google Maps frame so you can see where our office is; when that frame loads, Google receives the request and applies its own privacy policy to it. Some parts of the site remember small preferences in your own browser's local storage — that data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

6. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Enquiry details reach our team by email and are handled by the people working on your project. No transmission over the internet is ever completely secure, so please do not send confidential documents through the enquiry form.

7. Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the address or email below and we will respond.
